# Photo review — alt text and curation

Status: **alt text reviewed** against the actual images. Curation issues remain.

The photographs were downloaded from the parish's A Church Near You media library. Each one has
now been looked at and given alt text that describes what is actually shown, rather than a guess.

## What the review changed

Looking at the images showed several did not match how they were being used:

- `old-church-building.jpg` was a lower-resolution copy of `church-05.jpg`. The duplicate has been
  deleted and the page now uses the higher-resolution original.
- The news story about the North Aisle roof was illustrated with a photograph of New Orchard Farm.
  It now shows the north side of the church.
- The coffee morning was illustrated with an aerial view. It now uses the dusk photograph.
- The weddings page used a faded 1990s print. It now uses the dusk photograph.
- The image on the give page is two interior photographs of the damaged walls, not a flyer, and its
  alt text and caption now say so.

## Remaining curation issues

These need the parish, not a developer:

- **There is no photograph of people.** The site is entirely buildings and historical prints, which
  weakens the "everyone is welcome" message. A few photos of services, the toddler group or the
  Thursday mornings would transform it.
- `church-03.jpg` (New Orchard Farm) and `church-06.png` (original church) are historical documents
  rather than pictures of the church today. They suit the history page better than a gallery.
- `church-04.jpg` is badly overexposed and looks like a 1990s print. Worth replacing.
- Two images are named as if they were a "flyer" — the file name is now misleading.
- **Image rights:** these were taken from A Church Near You. Before any real use the parish should
  confirm they are happy for them to be republished, and ideally supply the originals.
